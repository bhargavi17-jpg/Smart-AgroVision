Observed Symptomatology (తెగులు లక్షణాలు)

Concentric circular dark brown spots with a distinct target-board pattern on mature lower foliage, surrounded by chlorotic yellow halos.

Organic & Cultural Controls

  • Prune infected bottom leaves up to 25cm from soil bed.
  • Apply cold-pressed Neem oil (5ml/L) with soap emulsifier.
  • Mulch soil with dried straw to block fungal splash.

Chemical & Bio-Sprays

  • Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5g/L of water.
  • Or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 3.0g/L.
  • Bio-control: Trichoderma viride @ 5g/L root drench.

Preventative Safeguards

  • Rotate crop with non-solanaceous species for 2 years.
  • Maintain balanced potassium and nitrogen levels.
  • Sterilize pruning shears between rows.
